# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

International B2B commodity and goods buyers, institutional procurement officers, global import partners, and sourcing directors across textiles, pharmaceuticals, building materials, agricultural commodities, and enterprise technology services. They evaluate suppliers in high-stakes transactions under tight deadlines, looking for verifiable operational capability, stable supply chains, and zero broker friction.

## Product Purpose

Establish immediate institutional authority and credibility for Trivoxa Group. Eliminate the suspicion of an unproven middleman by anchoring the brand directly to the manufacturing foundation of its parent company, Shiveshwar Textiles in Surat, Gujarat. Present verifiable export capabilities (exact ports, HS codes, capacities, Incoterms, and certifications) and convert serious trade buyers into high-confidence RFQs within 24 hours.

## Brand Personality

- **Voice & Tone**: Authoritative, precise, sovereign, sober, and architecturally disciplined.
- **3-Word Personality**: Sovereign, Rigorous, Architectural.
- **Emotional Goals**: Instill unshakeable confidence, calm institutional gravity, and prestige without arrogance.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-cream or AI-template marketing aesthetics.
- Startup purple/violet gradients, floaty blur circles, or decorative glassmorphism.
- Fake metric badges, unearned 5-star ratings, or gimmicky countdowns.
- Bouncy, cartoony, or whimsical micro-animations.
- Vague marketing claims that lack HS codes, verifiable specs, or physical operational reality.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims**: Every statement is anchored to verifiable data (ports, mill acreage, loom counts, HS codes, Incoterms).
2. **Restrained Luxury**: Bronze (#A88B68) is strictly a precious accent covering <=3% of any surface, never decorative clutter.
3. **Data-Typographic Discipline**: Serifs for classical permanence (Instrument Serif), high-legibility sans for prose (Satoshi / Inter), and monospace (Geist Mono) strictly for operational data.
4. **Graceful Degradation & Zero Jank**: 100% server-rendered final states, sub-2.5s LCP, zero layout shift, seamless fallback for low-tier hardware or reduced motion.
5. **Architectural Layout & Visible Structure**: Hairlines, deliberate rhythm, asymmetric confidence, and crisp spatial hierarchy over generic cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliant across both dark and light surfaces (tested contrast matrix >=4.5:1 for body copy, >=3:1 for large display elements).
- Strict adherence to `prefers-reduced-motion` with static poster fallbacks.
- Full keyboard operability and visible high-contrast focus rings.
- Semantic HTML and ARIA regions throughout the site.
